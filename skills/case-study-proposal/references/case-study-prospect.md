# Full case study, prospect

Title (h1): `[Customer] x Example Feedback: Case Study Guidelines`
Line under title: `[Month D, YYYY] · Key people: [contact] ([Customer]), [requester, role] (Example Feedback), [Case study owner name](mailto:case-study-owner@example.com) (Example Feedback)`. See "Key people line" in SKILL.md.

Intro line under that: "This is a starting idea based on what you've shared with us so far. Nothing here needs to be right yet. These are the things we may check in on once you're up and running."

## 1. Where the story could go (h2)
One sentence on what [Customer] plans to do with Example Feedback and what they hope to achieve. Then:
- Planned setup: [survey type and channel]
- Main goal: [the outcome the flow is built for]
- Analysis: [how feedback will be analyzed]
- Possible story: [one survey/flow could give them X and Y]
- What we'll check in on later: 2 or 3 questions tied to the topic, phrased as future check-ins (e.g. "Once the help desk CSAT flow is live, how is your team following up on responses?"). Never ask the prospect to confirm the story or say whether it's correct.

Keep pre-filled detail light. Use the deal context to pick the topic, not to write their answers for them. The doc should read as a plan with open questions, not a story they could already sign off on.

## 2. Metrics to track from day one (h2)
"Record a baseline before launch, then check monthly. Strong case studies show a before and after."

Table `Metric | What it shows | Your input`, 3 to 5 rows, each a before vs now comparison tied to the chosen topic. For G2/review topics, link "like here" to the Fabrikam story in the "What it shows" cell.

The "Your input" column is where the prospect adds their numbers, so every cell in it gets `background-color:#FFF4CC` and "✍️". If Hex has a baseline number, pre-fill it in that cell (keep it yellow, mark it `[to confirm]`). The "Metric" and "What it shows" columns stay white.

Close: "Also worth keeping: one or two quotes from the team on why [the flow] mattered." Never ask for screenshots.

For existing customers with results already, phrase metrics as results to confirm, not a baseline to start.

## 3. What the case study includes (h2)
"We write it, your team reviews and approves. Structure follows our [customer stories](https://example.com/customer-stories/), see [reference story](closest from Fixed assets) as a reference."

"We'll include:"
- Company snapshot: industry, company size, customer since, features used
- Headline numbers: 3 to 4 results shown at the top (for example: [two metrics from the table])
- Intro: who [Customer] is and what it does
- The challenge: [the specific gap, in their terms]
- The solution: how [features] connect, with a short how-it-works breakdown
- The result: before and after numbers from the metrics above
- Quotes: 3 to 5 quotes
- Visuals: product visuals prepared by us, plus your logo and a team photo

"What we need from you: metrics from the table above, quotes, logo, a team photo, and final approval. Interview may or may not be needed."

## 4. How we use the case study (h2)
"The story gets visibility for [Customer] across our website and LinkedIn. Every piece is shared with you for approval before it goes live."

Table `Where | What`, always these rows:
- [Customer stories](https://example.com/customer-stories/) | Full case study with a link to [Customer]
- Example Feedback website | Quotes and logo on relevant pages (landing pages, articles)
- Example Feedback LinkedIn company page | One post about the case study, tagging [Customer]
- Personal LinkedIn profile of someone at [Customer] (optional) | Optional: one post, drafted by us in English, ready to edit and publish ([example](LinkedIn example URL from Fixed assets)). + sponsored promotion of the personal LinkedIn post of someone at [Customer] (Example Feedback covers the costs)
