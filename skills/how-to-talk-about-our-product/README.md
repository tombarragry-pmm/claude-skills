# how-to-talk-about-our-product

A messaging guide for one product, packaged as a skill so every team describes it the same way: what it is, who it's for, the value props, approved phrases, terms to handle with care, objection handling and competitive framing.

Originally built: November 2026

## What it does

1. Gives Claude the official category, headline and one-liner to anchor any content.
2. Matches the angle to the audience: sales, CS, product, support, marketing or everyone else.
3. Supplies an approved phrase bank and persona taglines, so new copy reuses chosen language.
4. Flags risky terms and handles common objections, including "why not just use an LLM?"
5. Flags out-of-ICP audiences and asks about them before drafting.

## Files

- `SKILL.md`: the instructions Claude follows.

## Requirements

- None. Everything the skill needs is in `SKILL.md`.

## Make it yours

The examples use Example Feedback, a fictional customer feedback platform, and its research product, Feedback Library. Replace the product, the headline and phrases, the personas, the competitors (fictional here), the scenarios and the customer quote placeholder with your own.
