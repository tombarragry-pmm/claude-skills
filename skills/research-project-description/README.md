# research-project-description

Turns a short research hint (a use case, question or topic) into a ready-to-use project template for a research repository product: a title, a short description and a full, structured project description.

Originally built: November 2026

## What it does

1. Takes a brief hint such as "churn analysis" or "onboarding drop-off".
2. Writes a title (max 40 characters) and a short description (max 150 characters).
3. Writes a full project description with a cross-source opening paragraph, research objectives, themes and patterns to look for, insights to uncover, and how findings will be used.
4. Keeps the framing multi-source, names survey signals specifically and describes every other source by signal type rather than by tool.

## Files

- `SKILL.md`: the instructions Claude follows, including four example templates used as quality and tone references.

## Requirements

None.

## Make it yours

The examples use Research Projects, the research product area of Example Feedback, a fictional customer feedback platform. Replace the product and product area names with your own, adjust the length limits to your product's field limits, and swap in example templates from your own research use cases.
