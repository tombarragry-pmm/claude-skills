# tom-pmm-inapp-changelog

Writes short, emoji-led in-app changelog posts (the kind shown in a widget such as Beamer) with a fixed structure: tag, title, opening, benefit bullets, optional access note and a CTA button.

Originally built: November 2026

## What it does

1. Collects a brief: the update, its tag, what users can now do, plan or access caveats, and the outcome angle.
2. Drafts three title options and waits for approval.
3. Drafts the opening paragraph and waits for feedback.
4. Drafts the benefit bullets, optional access note and CTA together.
5. Assembles the approved parts into a final post ready to paste, or to hand to `tom-pmm-create-inapp-post`.

## Files

- `SKILL.md`: the instructions Claude follows.

## Requirements

None.

## Make it yours

The examples use Example Feedback, a fictional customer feedback platform. Replace the tags, product area names, plan names, retired names and persona names in the tone rules, and the source headline list with your own.
