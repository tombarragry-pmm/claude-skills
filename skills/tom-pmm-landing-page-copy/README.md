# tom-pmm-landing-page-copy

Writes and refines product landing page copy one section at a time, agreeing on the angle before drafting. It fixes the page structure, per-section copy rules and character limits, and a short design-direction format for the designer.

Originally built: November 2026

## What it does

1. Works through the page section by section, in a standard order (hero, logo banner, three feature sections, testimonial, capability cards, how it works, AI accordion, FAQ, closing CTA).
2. For each section, shows the current copy, gives an honest read of what is weak, and proposes angles.
3. Agrees the angle first, then drafts several short options to react to and combine.
4. Applies length limits and copy rules (problem-first, outcome-focused, no superlatives, no dashes, US English).
5. Adds 2 to 3 bullets of design direction per section: what the visual shows, the key moment, and what to avoid.

## Files

## Requirements

None.

## Make it yours

The examples use Example Feedback, a fictional customer feedback platform, and its fictional research product, Feedback Lab. Replace the product and feature names, the standard capability card set, the FAQ topics, the closing tagline, the internal persona codenames to avoid, and the list of retired feature names with your own.
